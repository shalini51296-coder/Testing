# Homeschool Curriculum, Age 2 to 10: Scope, Sequence and Weekly Plan

**Purpose.** This document answers the question: *if a child is homeschooled from age 2 to 10, what must she learn, in what order, how is the week organised, and which parts does the Tara app teach versus the parent and the real world?*

Its benchmarks come from three sources:
- **India:** NCF for School Education 2023, NCF-Foundational Stage 2022, NIPUN Bharat, and NCERT learning outcomes for Classes 1–5. These keep a homeschooled child level with or ahead of CBSE peers, so she can join or rejoin school at any point.
- **Research base:** docs 01–04.
- **International benchmarks for depth:** UK National Curriculum KS1–KS2 and US Common Core.

## 1. Honest coverage statement

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Does the app's content now reach age 10 (Class 5)? | **Yes for drill, practice and knowledge in maths, English, Hindi and EVS:** 205 skills across levels L0–L8. **No app alone is a complete education.** Writing, extended reading of real books, projects, art, music, physical activity and conversation must happen off-screen, and this plan schedules them. |
| What does the app do best? | Systematic, adaptive, spaced practice: phonics, number sense, arithmetic fluency, vocabulary, grammar, Hindi script and grammar, factual knowledge, and comprehension questions. |
| What must the parent provide? | About 30–60 minutes a day of the off-screen work (Section 4). At age 4 that is mostly reading aloud, talk and play. |

## 2. Scope and sequence by subject

Each cell lists the goal for that age. Off-screen work is shown in *italics*.

### 2.1 Language and literacy: English

| Age (level) | Oral language | Reading | Writing | Grammar and words |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2–3 (L0–L1) | Naming 50 → 300 words; 2-step instructions; nursery rhymes | *Picture books daily (dialogic reading)* | *Scribbling, crayons* | — |
| 3.5–4.5 (L2) | Categories, opposites, prepositions, story questions | Letters of own name; first sounds; rhyme | *Pre-writing strokes, own name* | — |
| 4.5–5.5 (L3) | Retelling; why/how questions | **Phonics groups 1–7; CVC reading**; tricky words | *All lowercase letters on paper*; spelling with tiles | — |
| 5.5–6.5 (L4, Class 1) | Tier-2 words ("Word power 1") | Vowel digraphs; decodable books; **Read & answer 1** | *Sentences with capital and full stop* | Plurals, a/an, end punctuation, opposites |
| 6.5–7.5 (L5, Class 2) | Word power 2 | Fluency (re-reading); **Read & answer 2** | *3–5 sentence recount or story* | Past tense, nouns/verbs, contractions, tense, synonyms |
| 7.5–8.5 (L6, Class 3) | Word power 3 | Non-fiction; inference; **Read & answer 3** | *Paragraphs; letters; diary* | Adjectives/pronouns, comparatives, homophones, prefixes |
| 8.5–9.5 (L7, Class 4) | Word power 4; idioms | Text structure; summarising; **Read & answer 4** | *Narrative, explanation, opinion; keyboarding starts* | All parts of speech, suffixes, roots |
| 9.5–10.5 (L8, Class 5) | Word power 5; debate | Compare texts; theme; research; **Read & answer 5** | *Multi-paragraph essays; editing* | Greek/Latin roots, advanced vocabulary |

**Real books (essential).** The app's passages are short. Fluency and love of reading come from books.
- **Ages 2–5:** read aloud 2–3 picture books a day.
- **Ages 5–7:** decodable readers, then early chapter books.
- **Ages 7–10:** 20+ minutes of independent reading daily, plus one family read-aloud.
- **Free sources:**
  - **Pratham Books / StoryWeaver** (free, in English and Hindi, levelled 1–4)
  - **NCERT textbooks** on ePathshala (free)
  - the public library

| 4.5–5.5 (L3) | Riddles, richer stories | स्वर, व्यंजन, first words without matras | — |
| 5.5–6.5 (L4) | Retelling | **Matras ा ि ी ु ू** | — |
| 6.5–7.5 (L5) | Poems | **Matras े ै ो ौ ं ँ**; **पढ़ो और समझो 1** | विलोम, लिंग, सप्ताह के दिन |
| 7.5–8.5 (L6) | Short speeches | **संयुक्त अक्षर**; reading stories | वचन, ऋतुएँ, गिनती 1–50 |

*Off-screen:* Hindi read-alouds (Panchatantra, Akbar-Birbal, StoryWeaver Hindi), writing on paper, and conversation. Because she speaks Hindi at home, daily speaking practice is already covered. Focus parent time on **reading and writing Devanagari**.

### 2.3 Mathematics

| Level | Number and place value | Operations | Fractions and decimals | Measurement, geometry, data |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| L0–L1 | One/many; subitize to 3; give-N to 3 | — | — | Shapes (circle, square, triangle); big/small; AB patterns |
| L2 | Count to 10 (cardinality); numerals to 10; compare | Put together/take away with objects | — | More shapes; sort and count |
| L3 | To 20; Number Race; teens; one more/less | Bonds to 5 and 10; story sums within 10 | — | ABC patterns; 3D shapes |
| L4 (Class 1) | Tens and ones; compare to 100; skip counting; even/odd | Within 20 by strategy; missing numbers | — | ₹ coins; o'clock and half past |
| L5 (Class 2) | Hundreds; number patterns | 2-digit +/− with regrouping; equal groups; tables 2, 5, 10; word problems | Halves, thirds, quarters | Shopping and change; time to 5 minutes; m and cm |
| L6 (Class 3) | Thousands; rounding; Roman numerals | 3-digit +/−; all tables; 2-digit × 1-digit; division facts and remainders | Unit fractions; equivalent fractions | Perimeter; calendar; kg/g, L/mL |
| L7 (Class 4) | **Lakhs (Indian commas)**; factors | Multi-digit ×; long division; × and ÷ word problems | Add like fractions; fraction of a quantity | Angles; area; 24-hour time |
| L8 (Class 5) | **Crores**; primes; LCM/HCF | Average; **unknowns (□)** | Decimals; fraction ↔ decimal ↔ percent | Volume; bar graphs |

*Off-screen (weekly):*
- Cooking (measuring)
- Shopping with real rupees
- Board games (Ludo and snakes-and-ladders are linear number boards)
- Tangrams and blocks
- Clock reading at home
- Measuring the room

| Level | Science units | Social-studies units |
|---|---|---|
| L2–L3 | Senses, animals and homes, plants' needs, weather, day/night, healthy habits | Family, helpers, festivals, road safety |
| L4 | Body organs, plant parts, herbivores/carnivores, seasons, materials | Capital, national symbols, flag, emergency number 112 |
| L5 | Food groups, sleep, seeds, desert and pond plants, metamorphosis, states of water, magnets | Ganga, national anthem, harvest festivals, 3 Rs |
| L6 | Bones, brain, digestion, photosynthesis, pollination, mammals/reptiles, migration, water cycle, solar system, states of matter, gravity | States and UTs, Himalayas, Thar, continents and oceans, Gandhiji, Independence Day |
| L7 | Blood circulation, eye, malaria, seed dispersal, food chains, endangered animals, simple machines, friction, conductors, planets | Peninsula, national motto and emblem, Republic Day, Constitution, Dandi March, panchayat, voting age |
| L8 | Kidneys, vaccines, nutrients, stomata, amphibians, adaptation, national parks, atmosphere, earthquakes, Chandrayaan-3, solutions, energy | Largest state, national song, C. V. Raman, Taj Mahal, Indus Valley, first PM, three organs of government, fundamental rights, climate |

*Off-screen (essential for science):*
- **Weekly hands-on experiment** following the app's Predict → Try → Observe → Explain loop (sprouting seeds, floating/sinking, shadows through the day, magnets, the water cycle in a bag).
- **Nature walk** with a sketch notebook.
- **Monthly outing:** museum, farm, planetarium, zoo, post office, railway station.

### 2.5 Thinking, executive function and computing

| Level | Skills |
|---|---|
| L0–L3 | Matching, sorting, odd-one-out, rotation, memory, go/no-go, rule switching (in app) |
| L4–L6 | *Unplugged coding (give a robot directions), ScratchJr (free), chess, Sudoku 4×4* |
| L7–L8 | *Scratch (free, MIT), logic puzzles, strategy games, typing practice; simple projects (make a quiz or animation)* |

### 2.6 Arts, music, movement and life skills (off-screen, but part of the curriculum)

These are not "extras". NCF-SE 2023 treats arts and physical education as core. Executive function improves most with activities that combine physical, social and emotional challenge (Diamond & Ling 2016).

| Area | Weekly target |
|---|---|
| **Physical** | WHO: **60+ minutes of active play daily** (ages 5–17). Yoga, cycling, swimming, dance, sports. |
| **Art and craft** | 2–3 sessions: drawing, clay, origami (also spatial skill), collage |
| **Music** | Singing daily; rhythm and clapping (phonological link); an instrument from about 6–7 if she is interested |
| **Life skills** | Dressing, tidying, simple cooking, money, gardening, caring for a plant or pet |
| **Social** | Weekly playdates or group activities. Homeschooled children need planned peer time. |
| **Values and feelings** | Stories (Panchatantra, Jataka), feelings talk, helping others |

## 3. Daily and weekly timetables

The principle is short, focused blocks; play-based before 6; never more than the WHO screen limits for her age.

### Age 4–5 (now): about 1.5–2 hours of "learning" spread across the day

| Time | Activity | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Morning, 15 min | **Tara session** (phonics, number, Hindi, thinking) | App |
| Morning, 20 min | Read-aloud: 2 picture books, using the app's dialogic-reading prompts | Parent |
| Morning, 30 min | Free play with blocks, puzzles, pretend play | Off-screen |
| Midday, 10 min | Writing practice: today's letter on a slate or paper, following the app's mission | Paper |
| Afternoon, 60 min+ | Outdoor play, movement, nature | Off-screen |
| Evening, 10 min | Hindi story or rhyme; counting during daily tasks | Parent |
| Weekly | 1 science experiment · 2 art sessions · 1 outing · 1–2 playdates | Off-screen |

### Ages 6–7 (Class 1–2): about 3 hours of structured learning

| Block | Minutes | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Maths | 30 (15 app + 15 hands-on) | App maths topics, then the same concept with objects |
| English | 40 (15 app + 25 reading/writing) | App phonics/grammar/vocabulary, then a real book and a few sentences of writing |
| Hindi | 30 (10 app + 20 reading/writing) | App matras/grammar, then a StoryWeaver Hindi book and writing |
| EVS | 30, 4×/week | App Curiosity Lab, then an experiment, outing or project |
| Arts/music | 30 | Rotating |
| Physical | 60+ | Daily |

### Ages 8–10 (Class 3–5): about 4 hours of structured learning

| Block | Minutes |
|---|---|
| Maths | 45 (20 app + 25 written problems in a notebook) |
| English | 50 (15 app + 20 independent reading + 15 writing) |
| Hindi | 40 (10 app + 15 reading + 15 writing) |
| EVS / science / social studies | 45 (15 app + 30 project, experiment or research) |
| Computing / logic | 20, 3×/week |
| Arts, music, sport, life skills | 60+ |

**App screen time stays within the daily cap set in Parent Corner:** about 15 min at 4–5, 30 at 6–7, and 45–60 at 8–10, split across subjects.

## 4. Assessment and records

- **Continuous:** the app's learner model (Parent Corner shows mastered / learning / struggling for each area).
- **Every 3 months:**
  - A parent checklist against Section 2 for the child's level.
  - A short reading sample: count words read correctly in 1 minute against the Hasbrouck & Tindal benchmarks in doc 03.
  - A writing sample filed in a portfolio.
- **Once a year:** NCERT end-of-class exemplar problems (free on the NCERT site) for maths, English, Hindi and EVS.
- **Portfolio:** photos of projects, writing samples, a books-read list and app progress exports. This is useful if she joins school, or for NIOS Open Basic Education records.

> Admission note: Indian schools usually test against the class syllabus. The level mapping above (L4 ≈ Class 1 … L8 ≈ Class 5) keeps her eligible for age-appropriate entry.

## 5. Gaps and roadmap

| Gap | Plan |
|---|---|
| Illustrations are emoji | Commission a consistent illustration set (about 800 images for L0–L4; quizzes for 6+ need fewer images) |
| Human voice | Recording Studio now; a voice artist later |
| Handwriting and tracing in app | Scheduled work block (finger tracing with stroke order, plus paper missions) |
| Talking storybooks with dialogic prompts (L1–L4) | Scheduled work block (12 original stories) |
| More passages and vocabulary | Grow to about 60 passages and 400+ words for L4–L8, with an audit of every item |
| Writing composition | Writing prompts and checklists in the app, with paper work reviewed by the parent |
| Science experiment guides | A printable "experiment of the week" guide for each EVS unit |
